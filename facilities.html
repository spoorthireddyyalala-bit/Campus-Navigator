Campus Facilities

Discover the facilities available for students, faculty and visitors at Bhoj Reddy Engineering College.

📚 Central Library

A well-equipped library with textbooks, journals, reference books and digital learning resources. Located in West Block First & Second Floors

💻 Computer Laboratories

Modern computer laboratories with high-speed systems for programming, practical sessions and project work.

🍽️ College Canteen

A hygienic canteen serving meals, snacks and beverages for students and staff. Located in East Block First Floor

🏀 Sports Facilities

Facilities for indoor and outdoor sports to encourage fitness and extracurricular activities.

📚 Stationary

Provides essential stationery items for students and staff. Located in East Block First Floor

🚗 Parking Area

Secure parking space for students, faculty members and visitors. Located in East Block First Floor

💼 Placement Cell

Provides career guidance, placement training, internship opportunities and campus recruitment support. Located in West Block Third Floor

🎓 Seminar Hall

Used for workshops, guest lectures, technical seminars and student presentations. Located in West Block Third Floor

🏛️ Auditorium

A spacious venue for cultural events, annual functions, conferences and college celebrations. Located in Ramdev Block RCC complex

Campus Highlights

Bhoj Reddy Engineering College provides a student-friendly learning environment with modern laboratories, a well-stocked library, seminar halls, a spacious auditorium, dedicated placement support, sports facilities and excellent infrastructure that contributes to academic excellence and overall student development.
